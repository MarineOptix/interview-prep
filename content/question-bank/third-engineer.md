# Third Engineer

## experience
- What machinery were you responsible for on your last ship?
- What generator and boiler types have you worked with?
- Tell me about a breakdown you repaired yourself.
- Have you worked on UMS ships as duty engineer?

## conventions
- What are the MARPOL Annex I rules for discharging bilge water?
- What must be recorded in the Oil Record Book?
- What are the boiler safety requirements and tests?
- What are the STCW hours of rest?
- What are the SOLAS requirements for the emergency generator?

## safety
- A fire breaks out in the engine room during your watch. What do you do?
- The boiler has a low water level alarm. What do you do?
- What do you do in case of a blackout on your watch?
- What precautions do you take before working on a generator?
- How do you prevent and react to a fuel oil leak on a hot surface?

## technical
- What are the main responsibilities of a Third Engineer?
- How do you put a generator in parallel with the running one?
- How do you prepare the main engine for departure?
- How do you carry out a generator overhaul (decarbonisation)?
- How do you test boiler water and what do the results mean?
- How do you maintain an air compressor?
- What do you check before leaving the engine room unmanned?

## leadership
- How do you hand over the watch to the next engineer?
- How do you work with the motorman and the fitter?
