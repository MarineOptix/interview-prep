# Engine Cadet

## motivation
- Why did you choose marine engineering?
- What do you expect from your cadetship?
- How do you feel about working in a hot and noisy engine room for months?

## experience
- What workshop practice or sea training have you done?
- What subjects did you like most at the maritime academy?
- What tools and machines can you already use?

## conventions
- What is MARPOL and why does it matter in the engine room?
- What is the Oil Record Book?
- What is STCW and what certificate will you work towards?
- What is the ISM Code in simple words?

## safety
- What are the main hazards in the engine room and how do you protect yourself?
- You see a fuel leak near a hot exhaust pipe. What do you do?
- What is lock-out and tag-out?
- What do you do when you hear the engine room fire alarm?
- What is an enclosed space and why is it dangerous?

## technical
- What is the difference between a two-stroke and a four-stroke diesel engine?
- How will you learn the engine room systems on board?
- What is the purpose of a purifier?
- How does a centrifugal pump work?
- What is the purpose of the lube oil system?
- What is the purpose of the training record book?

## leadership
- What will you do if you do not understand an instruction?
- How do you get on with people from other cultures?
