# Third Officer

## experience
- What were your duties as Third Officer or cadet on your last ship?
- What life-saving and fire-fighting equipment were you responsible for?
- Describe a situation on your watch where you had to take action to avoid collision.
- What did you learn from your cadetship that you use now?

## conventions
- How do you know whether a risk of collision exists (COLREG Rule 7)?
- What are the duties of the give-way and stand-on vessels (Rules 16 and 17)?
- What are the SOLAS requirements for weekly and monthly inspections of life-saving appliances?
- How often must fire and abandon ship drills be held under SOLAS?
- What are the STCW hours of rest?
- What lights and shapes does a vessel not under command show?

## safety
- A crew member falls overboard during your watch. What do you do?
- When do you call the Master?
- What do you do if you discover a fire during your watch?
- How do you check a lifeboat before a drill?
- What do you do if the steering fails on your watch?

## technical
- What do you check when you take over the navigational watch?
- How do you fix the ship's position, and how often?
- How do you use radar and ARPA to assess a target?
- What is a Williamson turn and when is it used?
- How do you maintain lifeboats, life rafts and immersion suits?
- How do you test the EPIRB and SART?
- How do you take over hand steering from autopilot?

## leadership
- How do you work with the lookout and helmsman on your watch?
- How do you challenge the Master or pilot if you think something is wrong?
