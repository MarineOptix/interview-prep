# Second Engineer

## experience
- What main engine overhauls have you carried out?
- What main engine types and auxiliary machinery have you worked with?
- Tell me about a difficult breakdown at sea and how you repaired it.
- Have you worked with electronically controlled engines?
- Are you ready for promotion to Chief Engineer? Why?

## conventions
- What must be recorded in the Oil Record Book, and who makes the entries?
- What are the MARPOL Annex VI requirements for fuel changeover?
- What does the ISM Code require for critical equipment and maintenance?
- What are the requirements for testing the emergency generator and emergency fire pump?
- What are the STCW hours of rest for engineers and how do you plan around them?

## safety
- The oil mist detector alarms. What do you do?
- What are the signs of a scavenge fire and what do you do?
- What is your procedure for lock-out and tag-out before maintenance?
- How do you prepare for a main engine unit overhaul safely?
- What do you do in case of a fire in the purifier room?
- How do you manage the risk of a crankcase explosion?

## technical
- How do you plan the daily work of the engine room?
- Describe how you carry out a main engine unit overhaul.
- How do you take and use crankshaft deflection readings?
- How do you measure liner wear and piston ring clearances?
- How do you manage critical equipment and spare parts?
- How does an electronically controlled engine (ME-C) differ from a camshaft engine (MC)?
- How do you maintain the turbocharger?

## leadership
- How do you organise the engine crew and delegate work?
- How do you train junior engineers?
- How do you report to the Chief Engineer?
