# Ordinary Seaman (OS)

## motivation
- Why do you want to work as an OS?
- What are your plans to become AB?

## experience
- What sea service or training do you have?
- What work have you done on deck?
- Have you taken part in mooring or cargo operations?
- What did you learn from the Bosun and ABs on your last ship?

## conventions
- What are your rest hours under STCW and MLC?
- What is the garbage segregation on board?
- What is the purpose of the ship's security watch?

## safety
- You discover a fire on board. What do you do?
- What do you do when you hear the general alarm?
- What precautions do you take when chipping and painting?
- What PPE do you use on deck?
- Where is your muster station and what are your duties?

## technical
- Which knots do you know and where are they used?
- How do you keep a lookout?
- How do you help during mooring?
- How do you clean and paint a surface?
- How do you use a fire extinguisher?

## leadership
- How do you make sure you get enough rest on board?
- How do you follow instructions from the Bosun?
