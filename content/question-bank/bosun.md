# Bosun

## experience
- How many years have you worked as Bosun or AB, and on which vessel types?
- What deck maintenance projects have you organised?
- Tell me about a difficult mooring operation you were involved in.
- How many crew members have you supervised?

## conventions
- What is a permit to work and when is it required?
- What are your duties under the ship's security plan?
- What is the garbage management plan, and how does the deck crew follow it?
- What does MARPOL say about oil on deck and scuppers?

## safety
- What are the main safety rules during mooring operations?
- What precautions do you take for working aloft or over the side?
- What do you check before an enclosed space entry as the standby person?
- How do you inspect mooring lines and wires?
- How do you rig a pilot ladder safely?
- What do you do if a crew member is injured on deck?

## technical
- How do you organise the daily work of the deck crew?
- How do you manage deck stores and paint?
- How do you prepare the deck for heavy weather?
- How do you maintain winches, windlass and mooring equipment?
- How do you prepare surfaces before painting?
- How do you secure cargo or deck equipment for sea?

## leadership
- A crew member keeps ignoring the safety rules. How do you deal with it?
- How do you train new ABs and OSs?
- How do you report to the Chief Officer?
- How do you motivate the crew during hard work in bad weather?
