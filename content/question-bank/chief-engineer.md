# Chief Engineer

## experience
- What main engine types have you been responsible for as Chief or Second Engineer?
- Tell me about the most serious machinery breakdown you managed and how you solved it.
- How did you prepare for and manage a dry dock?
- What results have you achieved in fuel consumption or maintenance costs?
- Have you worked with electronically controlled engines (ME-C, RT-flex, X-engines)?

## conventions
- What are the MARPOL Annex VI sulphur limits globally and in ECAs?
- What are EEXI, CII and SEEMP, and what is the Chief Engineer's role?
- What must be recorded in the Oil Record Book Part I?
- What is required for the oily water separator and 15 ppm alarm?
- What are the requirements for bunker delivery notes and MARPOL fuel samples?
- What does the ISM Code require for critical equipment?
- What are the requirements of the Ballast Water Management Convention for the engine room?

## safety
- The ship has a blackout in a narrow channel. What do you do?
- What are the causes of a crankcase explosion and how do you prevent it?
- How do you prepare the engine room for the release of the fixed CO2 system?
- How do you control the risk of fuel spray fires in the engine room?
- How do you manage hot work in the engine room?
- What would you do if a junior engineer suggests bypassing the OWS?

## technical
- How do you control fuel consumption and the ship's energy efficiency?
- How do you change over to compliant fuel before entering an ECA?
- How do you analyse main engine performance?
- What is your approach to the planned maintenance system and spare parts?
- How do you deal with poor quality bunker fuel?
- How do you prepare the engine department for PSC or a class survey?
- How do you manage the engine department budget?

## leadership
- How do you manage and develop your engineers?
- How do you work with the Master on decisions that affect the engine room?
- How do you handle an engineer who repeatedly does not follow procedures?
- How do you communicate with the superintendent ashore?
