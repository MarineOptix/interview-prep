# Cook

## experience
- How many crew have you cooked for, and of which nationalities?
- What cuisines can you cook?
- What vessel types have you worked on as Cook?
- Tell me about a difficult situation with provisions on a long voyage.

## conventions
- What does MLC 2006 require for food and catering on board?
- What does MARPOL Annex V say about food waste?
- Who inspects the galley and provisions on board, and how often?
- What certificates does a ship's cook need?

## safety
- Oil in a pan catches fire in the galley. What do you do?
- How do you prevent food poisoning on board?
- What are the main hazards in the galley?
- What do you do if a crew member has a food allergy?
- How do you secure the galley for heavy weather?

## technical
- How do you plan menus for a multinational crew?
- How do you make sure the food you prepare is safe?
- How do you manage provisions and stores?
- What are the correct temperatures for storing and cooking food?
- How do you keep within the daily feeding budget?
- How do you prepare halal or vegetarian meals?
- How do you prevent cross-contamination?

## leadership
- A crew member complains about the food. How do you handle it?
- How do you work with and train the steward?
