# Chief Officer

## experience
- What cargo operations have you planned and supervised?
- What vessel types have you served on as Chief Officer or senior officer?
- Tell me about a cargo or ballast operation that went wrong and what you did.
- How did you prepare for a vetting or PSC inspection as Chief Officer?
- Are you ready for promotion to Master? Why?

## conventions
- How does your ship comply with the Ballast Water Management Convention?
- What are the main requirements of the IMSBC Code for solid bulk cargoes?
- What does ISGOTT say about the ship-shore safety checklist?
- What are the requirements for enclosed space entry and rescue drills under SOLAS?
- What is the Cargo Securing Manual and when do you use it?
- What are the responsibilities of the Ship Security Officer, if you are the SSO?
- What are the requirements of the Load Line Convention for the Chief Officer?

## safety
- You are the responsible officer for an enclosed space entry. What is your procedure?
- How do you organise a fire-fighting party as the on-scene commander?
- What do you do if you notice the ship listing during cargo operations?
- How do you prevent mooring accidents?
- What precautions do you take before hot work on deck?
- What is your role during an oil spill on deck?

## technical
- What is the free surface effect and how do you control it?
- How do you calculate the GM, and what are the intact stability criteria?
- How do you control shear forces and bending moments during loading?
- How do you carry out a draft survey?
- How do you prepare cargo holds or tanks for a new cargo?
- How do you check hatch cover weathertightness?
- On tankers: how does the inert gas system work and what oxygen level is required?
- How do you plan deck maintenance with the planned maintenance system?

## leadership
- How do you manage the Bosun and the deck crew?
- How do you train junior officers and cadets?
- How do you handle a junior officer who makes repeated mistakes on cargo watch?
