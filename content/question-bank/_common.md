# Common interview questions for all ranks

Format: `## <topic-id>` (motivation, experience, conventions, safety, technical, leadership, contract),
then one question per `- ` line. Rank files (`<slug>.md`) are added on top of these.

## motivation
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want to join our company?
- Why are you leaving your current or last company?
- Why are you interested in this vessel type?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- What do you know about our company and fleet?
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Why should we choose you over other candidates?

## experience
- Walk me through your sea service, starting with your last contract.
- Describe the most difficult situation you faced on board and how you handled it.
- Tell me about a mistake you made at sea and what you learned from it.
- What was your best achievement on your last contract?

## leadership
- How do you work in a multinational crew?
- Tell me about a conflict on board and how you resolved it.
- What do you do if a senior officer gives you an order you think is unsafe?
- How do you handle fatigue and stress on a long contract?
- How do you make sure an instruction is understood correctly?
- How do you report a near miss, and why is it important?
- What do you do in your free time on board?

## contract
- When are you available to join?
- What contract length and rotation do you prefer?
- What salary do you expect?
- Do you have a valid passport, seaman's book and US visa?
- Would you accept a shorter or longer contract if the company needs it?
- Do you have any questions for us?
