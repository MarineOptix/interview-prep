# Electro-Technical Officer (ETO)

## experience
- What electrical systems and voltages have you worked with?
- What automation and PLC systems have you maintained?
- Tell me about the most difficult electrical fault you found and repaired.
- Have you worked with high-voltage systems or electric propulsion?
- Have you been responsible for reefer containers?

## conventions
- What are the STCW requirements for an ETO (regulation III/6)?
- What are the SOLAS requirements for the emergency source of power?
- What are the requirements for testing the emergency generator and batteries?
- What are the requirements for navigation equipment that the ETO supports?

## safety
- What safety procedure do you follow before working on high-voltage equipment?
- How do you make sure equipment is dead before you work on it?
- What do you do if a crew member gets an electric shock?
- What precautions do you take in the battery room?
- How do you fight an electrical fire?

## technical
- How do you find an earth fault on the ship's network?
- How do you measure insulation resistance and what values are acceptable?
- How do you troubleshoot a fault in an automation or PLC system?
- What are your duties with refrigerated containers?
- How do you make sure the emergency power supply is ready?
- How does a power management system share load between generators?
- How do you maintain electric motors and starters?
- How do you troubleshoot a fault in the main engine control system?

## leadership
- How do you work with the engineers when a fault affects the whole engine room?
- How do you keep records of electrical maintenance?
