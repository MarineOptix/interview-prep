# Master / Captain

## experience
- What vessel types and sizes have you commanded or served on as Chief Officer?
- How did you prepare for your first command?
- Describe a difficult decision you made as Master and how you communicated it to the company.
- Tell me about a Port State Control or vetting inspection on your ship and its result.
- How have you handled a commercial pressure from charterers that conflicted with safety?

## conventions
- What does the Master's overriding authority mean under the ISM Code?
- What are the Master's responsibilities under the ISPS Code?
- What are the Master's duties under MLC 2006 regarding hours of rest, complaints and repatriation?
- What documents and certificates must be on board, and which ones would PSC check first?
- What are the Master's obligations to render assistance to persons in distress at sea (SOLAS V/33)?
- What is required for the Master's review of the Safety Management System?
- How do you ensure compliance with MARPOL record books on your ship?

## safety
- Your vessel runs aground. What are your immediate actions?
- What do you do after a collision?
- How do you handle a serious injury or a death on board?
- A crew member is found under the influence of alcohol or drugs. What do you do?
- How do you prepare the ship for heavy weather?
- What would you do in case of a piracy threat in a high-risk area?
- How do you make drills realistic and useful?

## technical
- When would you take over the conn from a pilot?
- How do you check and approve a passage plan?
- How do you manage the ship's stability and stress during cargo operations?
- What is your approach to anchoring in congested anchorages and in bad weather?
- How do you manage the ship's budget and reports to the company?
- How do you prepare for a company audit or class survey?

## leadership
- How do you build a safety culture on board?
- How do you handle a Chief Officer or Chief Engineer who disagrees with you?
- How do you evaluate and develop your officers?
- How do you deal with a crew member who files a complaint against an officer?
