# Fourth Engineer

## experience
- What were your responsibilities as Fourth Engineer or engine cadet?
- What purifier types and auxiliary machinery have you worked with?
- Tell me about a problem with a purifier or pump that you solved.
- Have you worked on UMS ships and been duty engineer?
- What main engine overhauls have you assisted with?

## conventions
- Someone asks you to pump bilge water overboard bypassing the oily water separator. What do you do?
- What must be recorded in the Oil Record Book Part I?
- What are the MARPOL Annex VI requirements for fuel samples during bunkering?
- What is the sulphur limit in an Emission Control Area?
- What are the STCW hours of rest?

## safety
- What do you check before you leave the engine room unmanned at night?
- What do you do if you find a fuel leak on a hot surface?
- What is the procedure before opening a purifier?
- What do you do if a fire starts in the purifier room?
- What are the dangers during bunkering and how do you prevent a spill?

## technical
- What are the main responsibilities of a Fourth Engineer?
- Your fuel oil purifier is overflowing through the water outlet. What do you do?
- How does a purifier work, and what is the gravity disc for?
- What is the Fourth Engineer's role during bunkering?
- How do you take soundings and calculate fuel quantities?
- How do you transfer fuel between tanks safely?
- How do you maintain an air compressor?
- What is the difference between a purifier and a clarifier?

## leadership
- How do you hand over the watch?
- How do you work with the Second Engineer and the motormen?
