# Able Seaman (AB)

## experience
- What vessel types have you worked on as AB?
- What were your duties on watch and on deck on your last ship?
- Tell me about a mooring operation you took part in.

## conventions
- What are your duties as gangway watch under the ISPS Code?
- What are the security levels and what changes at each level?
- What are your rest hours under STCW and MLC?
- What garbage can never be thrown overboard?

## safety
- What are your duties in an abandon ship drill?
- What do you do if you discover a fire?
- What precautions do you take during mooring?
- What do you do if someone falls overboard?
- What do you check before working aloft?

## technical
- How do you respond to helm orders and change over to hand steering?
- What is a good lookout, and how do you report a target?
- How do you rig a pilot ladder?
- How do you take soundings of tanks and bilges?
- What knots and splices do you know?
- How do you handle mooring lines on a drum end?
- How do you launch a lifeboat or rescue boat?

## leadership
- How do you work with the Bosun and the OS?
- What do you do if you do not understand an order?
