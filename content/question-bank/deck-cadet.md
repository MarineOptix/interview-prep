# Deck Cadet

## motivation
- Why did you choose a career at sea?
- Why do you want to be a deck officer rather than an engineer?
- What do you expect from your cadetship?
- How do you feel about spending many months away from home?

## experience
- What training ship or sea practice have you done?
- What subjects did you like most at the maritime academy?
- What practical skills have you already learned?

## conventions
- What is STCW and why is it important for you?
- What is the purpose of SOLAS and MARPOL?
- What is the ISM Code in simple words?
- What lights does a power-driven vessel underway show?
- What is the general emergency alarm signal?

## safety
- You hear the general emergency alarm. What do you do?
- What PPE do you wear on deck and why?
- What do you do if you see a fire?
- What is an enclosed space and why is it dangerous?
- What do you do if you see someone fall overboard?

## technical
- How do you report a target when you are on lookout?
- How do you plot a position on the chart?
- What is the difference between true and relative bearing?
- What are the main parts of a lifeboat?
- What knots do you know?
- What is the purpose of the training record book?

## leadership
- What will you do if you do not understand an instruction from an officer?
- How do you get on with people from other cultures?
