# Deck department — technical reference for interview answers

General reference only. Facts about the candidate come exclusively from their resume. Match the depth to the rank: management level (Master, Chief Officer), operational level (Second and Third Officer), support level (Bosun, AB, OS), cadets.

## Navigation and watchkeeping
- Passage planning in four stages: appraisal, planning, execution, monitoring (IMO Res. A.893). Berth-to-berth plan, no-go areas, safety contours, parallel indexing, abort points and contingencies.
- ECDIS: safety contour and safety depth settings, alarms, route check, updates (ENC permits, T&P notices), back-up arrangements.
- Watch handover: position, course and speed, traffic, weather, navigational warnings, standing and night orders, equipment status.
- Bridge Navigational Watch Alarm System (BNWAS), look-out duties, calling the Master (when in doubt, call the Master).
- COLREGs application, use of radar/ARPA (CPA/TCPA, trial manoeuvre), restricted visibility.
- Pilotage: Master–pilot information exchange, pilot ladder arrangements (SOLAS V/23).
- GMDSS: sea areas A1–A4, DSC distress alerts, EPIRB, SART/AIS-SART, NAVTEX; Second Officer usually responsible for GMDSS equipment and navigation charts and publications.

## Stability and cargo (officers)
- Metacentric height GM, KG, free surface effect, list vs loll, intact stability criteria, loading computer, draft survey.
- Chief Officer: cargo planning and supervision, stability and stress (shear forces and bending moments), ballast water management, cargo securing, hold/tank cleaning, deck maintenance planning, safety officer duties on many companies.
- Bulk carriers: IMSBC Code, grain loading, hold cleaning, hatch cover weathertightness, liquefaction risk (TML and moisture content).
- Tankers: cargo and ballast operations, inert gas system (oxygen content not above 8% by volume in cargo tanks), crude oil washing, venting, ship-shore safety checklist (ISGOTT), static electricity precautions, cargo calculations (ASTM tables, VCF).
- Container ships: bay plans, lashing, reefer monitoring, dangerous goods segregation (IMDG Code), weight verification (VGM).
- Gas carriers: IGC Code, cargo containment, boil-off, cool-down and gas-freeing.

## Ratings (Bosun, AB, OS)
- Steering by hand and change-over to manual, helm orders, look-out reports (bearings in points or degrees).
- Mooring operations: line handling, snap-back zones, winch operation, stoppers, heaving lines.
- Gangway and pilot ladder rigging, security watch duties.
- Deck maintenance: chipping and painting, lifeboat and LSA maintenance, rigging, cargo gear, working aloft and over the side with permits.
- Bosun: organising the deck crew’s daily work, toolbox talks, store management, reporting to the Chief Officer.

## Cadets
- Training record book, familiarisation, watchkeeping under supervision, basic chartwork and position fixing, willingness to learn.
