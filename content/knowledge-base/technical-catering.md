# Catering department — technical reference for interview answers

General reference only. Facts about the candidate come exclusively from their resume.

## MLC 2006 requirements (Regulation 3.2)
- Food and drinking water of appropriate quality, nutritional value and quantity, considering cultural and religious backgrounds of the crew; provided free of charge.
- Ship’s cooks must be trained and qualified (ship’s cook certificate where required by the flag); minimum age for a ship’s cook is 18.
- Frequent documented inspections of food and water supplies, storage areas and galley by or under the authority of the Master.

## Food safety and hygiene
- HACCP principles: identify hazards, critical control points, limits, monitoring, corrective actions, verification, records.
- Temperatures (typical company practice): refrigerated storage 0–5 °C, frozen storage −18 °C or below, hot holding above 63 °C, cooking core temperature at least 75 °C.
- Prevent cross-contamination: separate boards and knives (colour coding), raw and cooked food separated, hand washing, personal hygiene, illness reporting.
- First in, first out (FIFO) stock rotation, labelling and dating of opened food.
- Cleaning schedules, pest control, garbage segregation under MARPOL Annex V (food waste rules depend on distance from land and special areas).

## Provisions and budget
- Daily feeding rate / victualling budget per person, menu planning for the voyage, ordering provisions with the Master, checking deliveries, inventories.
- Planning for long passages and limited port calls.

## Multicultural crew
- Religious and cultural dietary needs (halal, no pork, vegetarian), variety, crew feedback, celebrations on board.

## Safety in the galley
- Galley fires (fat/oil fires: switch off heat, fire blanket, wet chemical extinguisher, never water), hot surfaces, sharp tools, securing equipment and stores for sea, slippery floors.
- Emergency duties from the muster list.

## Steward duties
- Cleaning of accommodation and public spaces, laundry, assisting the cook, mess room service, stores, hygiene in pantries.
