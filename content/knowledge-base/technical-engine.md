# Engine department — technical reference for interview answers

General reference only. Facts about the candidate come exclusively from their resume. Match the depth to the rank: management level (Chief and Second Engineer), operational level (Third and Fourth Engineer, ETO), support level (Motorman/Oiler), cadets.

## Main engine
- Two-stroke slow-speed engines (MAN B&W ME/MC, WinGD/Wärtsilä RT-flex/X), four-stroke medium-speed engines on smaller ships.
- Electronically controlled engines: hydraulic cylinder units, fuel and exhaust valve actuation, control system alarms.
- Common checks: scavenge space inspection (scavenge fires: causes and actions), crankcase explosion prevention (oil mist detector, relief valves, do not open the crankcase before cooling for the required time), bearing temperatures, cylinder lubrication.
- Overhauls: piston, fuel injector and exhaust valve overhauls, tightening torques and hydraulic jacks, clearances and crankshaft deflection readings.

## Auxiliary systems
- Generators and load sharing, blackout recovery and emergency generator starting.
- Purifiers (gravity disc selection, sludge discharge), fuel oil system, bunkering procedure and bunker sampling (MARPOL sample), switching to compliant low-sulphur fuel before entering an ECA.
- Boilers and steam plant, exhaust gas economiser, water treatment.
- Fresh water generator, sewage treatment plant, incinerator, oily water separator and the Oil Record Book (no magic pipes; tampering is a serious criminal offence).
- Steering gear tests before departure, emergency steering drills (every three months).
- Air compressors and starting air system, refrigeration and air conditioning (refrigerants, F-gas handling).
- Ballast water treatment systems.

## Watchkeeping and UMS
- Engine room watch handover, rounds, logbook, alarms; unmanned machinery space (UMS) checks before leaving the engine room, duty engineer, dead-man alarm.

## Maintenance and management
- Planned maintenance system (PMS), critical equipment, spare parts inventory, running hours, condition monitoring.
- Chief Engineer: fuel and lube oil consumption reporting, budgets, dry-dock specifications, class surveys, PSC readiness.
- Second Engineer: daily work planning for the engine crew, main engine overhauls, PMS execution.
- Third/Fourth Engineer: generators, purifiers, boilers, compressors, pumps as assigned by the company.

## Electrical (ETO)
- Main and emergency switchboards, insulation resistance testing, earth faults, high-voltage safety (permit, isolation, earthing, testing for dead), PLC and automation, alarm and monitoring systems, navigation equipment support, reefer containers.

## Ratings (Motorman / Oiler)
- Rounds and readings, lubrication, cleaning purifiers and filters, assisting overhauls, bunkering assistance, tank soundings, safe use of tools and lifting gear in the engine room.

## Cadets
- Training record book, learning systems by tracing pipelines, watch under supervision, assisting maintenance.
